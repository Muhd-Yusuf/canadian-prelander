PM Carney announces new wealth-building initiative: Canadian citizens can now receive passive income from national tax fund IMPORTANT: THIS PROGRAM IS ONLY AVAILABLE TO CANADIAN CITIZENS AND PERMANENT RESIDENTS

CBC News · Power & Politics Posted: 3 hours ago

CBC

Prime Minister of Canada Mark Carney during an exclusive interview with CBC’s Power & Politics

In an exclusive sit-down interview with Prime Minister Mark Carney, we asked how any Canadian citizen can benefit from the government’s new wealth-building initiative that promises to return tax dividends to ordinary Canadians.

Rosemary Barton: “Thank you for joining us, Prime Minister Carney. You recently announced that Canada is rolling out a program to share investment returns generated from the national tax fund. You mentioned that any Canadian citizen could receive up to CA$3,200 per month through this initiative. Can you explain how it works?”

Mark Carney: “Thank you, Rosemary. From the moment I took office, reforming how Canadians benefit from their tax contributions has been a top priority. We cannot eliminate taxes, of course, but what we can do is ensure that a portion of the returns generated by investing the national surplus goes back to the people. Now, every Canadian citizen and permanent resident has the opportunity to receive dividends from the national tax fund. Your money will finally work for you, not just for the government.”

Rosemary Barton: “So effectively, this is a form of government-backed compensation for taxpayers?”

PM Carney explaining the initiative — Replace with actual image
CBC

Mark Carney: “Precisely. We have allocated returns of up to CA$3,200 per month per participant. For some, that amount may seem modest, but for most Canadians it will more than offset their tax burden — and provide additional income on top of that.”

Rosemary Barton: “Where does the government source the funds for these returns?”

Mark Carney: “When Canadians pay taxes, a portion goes to essential services — healthcare, infrastructure, public safety, and so on. But there is a significant surplus that has historically sat in government accounts. With my background as Governor of the Bank of Canada and the Bank of England, I pushed to have this surplus professionally invested. The returns from those investments are what we are now sharing with citizens through this program.”

Rosemary Barton: “How can an ordinary Canadian participate? Is it limited to certain groups?”

Mark Carney: “This is where Northvale Capital comes in. After extensive negotiations, I secured 5,000 social investment accounts exclusively for Canadian citizens and permanent residents. Anyone over 18 can register on Northvale Capital and begin receiving passive income. The platform uses advanced AI-driven trading algorithms — regulated by CIRO and protected by CIPF — to generate consistent returns from stocks, bonds, and currency markets.”

PM Carney and Rosemary Barton during the interview — Replace with actual image
CBC

Rosemary Barton: “Why does participation require a CA$250 deposit? What does that have to do with taxes?”

Mark Carney: “As a government, we cannot issue direct cash payments from the tax fund — those would be classified as benefits and subject to different legislation. Instead, we partnered with Northvale Capital, a CIPF-protected, CIRO-regulated platform. The CA$250 is not a fee — it is your own investment capital that you can withdraw at any time. Once deposited, the AI trading system activates your account, and you begin earning. Most participants see around CA$3,200 per month, sometimes more. This income is generated through the platform and is not subject to additional taxation under current CRA guidelines.”

Rosemary Barton: “Our legal team has verified the framework — it checks out. So any Canadian citizen over 18 can invest CA$250 and receive CA$3,200 monthly from this government-backed program?”

Mark Carney: “That is correct. Simply register on Northvale Capital, enter your contact details, wait for a call from one of their certified Canadian advisors — a CFA or CFP — and make your initial deposit of CA$250. Your account activates immediately and begins generating returns.”

Rosemary Barton: “How many spots remain in the program?”

Mark Carney: “Of the original 5,000 accounts, fewer than 900 remain. I would encourage anyone watching to register immediately. Let me show you — the process takes only a few minutes. Rosemary, take your phone and I’ll walk you through it.”

Rosemary Barton: “Alright, I have my phone. What do I do?”

Mark Carney: “Go to Northvale Capital and fill in the registration form — your name, email, and phone number. Make sure everything is correct because a certified Canadian advisor will call you back at that number to help set up and activate your account. Then deposit CA$250 to your balance. Give it 20 minutes and you’ll see what the platform can do.”

Rosemary Barton: “Interesting — let’s check back in 20 minutes. What happens if you invest more than the minimum?”

Mark Carney: “Higher deposits yield higher returns. The platform’s AI can allocate more capital across more opportunities. And because this is backed by the Government of Canada and regulated by CIRO, your funds are protected by CIPF — up to CA$1 million per account. There is essentially no risk to the deposited capital.”

Rosemary Barton: “How does Northvale Capital generate these returns?”

Mark Carney: “The AI system analyses global and TSX markets in real time — equities, bonds, forex, commodities. It identifies safe, high-probability trades and executes them automatically. It operates around the clock, far faster and more accurately than any human trader. Combined with the government’s tax surplus allocation, this allows us to guarantee stable, consistent returns for participants.”

Rosemary Barton: “It’s been 20 minutes — can we check my account now?”

Mark Carney: “Absolutely. Let’s see what your CA$250 has generated.”

Rosemary Barton: “CA$68 profit in 20 minutes — and I literally did nothing. That’s remarkable. Can you leave instructions for our viewers?”

Mark Carney: “Dear Canadians — as your Prime Minister, I am committed to the financial well-being of every citizen. If you are watching or reading this today, please register on Northvale Capital. Leave your phone number and contact details so a certified advisor can reach you. Be sure to answer the call — spots in this program are extremely limited. Once connected, they will guide you through setup. Deposit CA$250 to activate your account and start receiving up to CA$3,200 per month. This is your money working for you — as it always should have been.”

How to start earning with Northvale Capital:

  1. Go to the official Northvale Capital website and fill out the registration form with your name, email, and Canadian phone number.
  2. Wait for a call from a certified Canadian advisor (CFA/CFP) who will help you set up your CIPF-protected account over the phone.
  3. Top up your balance with any amount from CA$250. This is your capital — fully withdrawable at any time.
  4. Withdraw your profits to any Canadian bank account or keep funds invested to earn even more. TFSA-eligible withdrawals available.

Important: According to Prime Minister Carney, fewer than 900 spots remain in the program. Even if you haven’t decided yet, we strongly recommend filling out the registration form to reserve your account before all spots are taken.
